# About

## The story so far

I'm a self-taught full-stack developer from Alcoy, Spain with 2 years of experience. My goal is to learn something new every day, and to build things that are useful to people.

I learn by doing. Every project teaches me something and that for me is the most important part of being a developer - you are always learning.

When I'm not coding, I'm learning piano, playing football or at the gym. All of these taught me the same lesson, show up every day, even when it feels like nothing's happening. Each session compounds. Results take time, but they come.

It's the same with coding. You don't learn a framework in a week. You write bad code, then slightly less bad code, and one day you look back and realise how far you've come.
